The Story

Where the hills breathe tea

Munnar sits where three streams meet in the high Western Ghats, and its slopes are stitched with tea estates first planted in the British era. Mornings arrive wrapped in fog; by noon the sun paints every ridge a different shade of green.

This journal gathers the places I visited, what made each one special, and the small practical lessons I would pass on to a friend planning the same trip.

From my travel notes
Travel Tips

Before you go

Pack layers

Mornings and nights are cold; carry a light rain jacket year-round.

Start early

Viewpoints are clearest at sunrise, and fog often rolls in by mid-morning.

Book ahead

Eravikulam tickets and good stays sell out on weekends and holidays.

Buy local

Fresh tea, spices and homemade chocolates make the best souvenirs.
